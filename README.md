## Overview

GetAIPhotoEditor is a browser-based AI photo editor. Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image, describe the change in plain language, and generate an edit with free credits — no app to install.

Start with 10 guest credits without an account (enough for one medium-quality edit). Sign in for 10 more credits and to keep results in My Creations. One-time credit packs are available when you need more.

Use it for product cutouts, portrait retouching, object cleanup, background changes, and creative restyling. Your original photo stays intact in the browser until you generate; failed or canceled jobs restore credits automatically.

## How to Edit a Photo With AI

1. **Upload your photo** — Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image up to 10 MB. No account needed to start.
2. **Describe the change** — Try “remove the background”, “make it look like golden hour”, or “clear acne while keeping the face natural”.
3. **Generate and download** — Inspect the result, then download or refine. A medium-quality edit uses 10 credits; other qualities cost more or less.

Works in a modern browser on iPhone, Android, Mac, or Windows.

## FAQ

**Is this AI photo editor really free?**  
Yes to start. You get 10 free credits without signing in (enough for one medium-quality edit). Sign in and you get 10 more. One-time credit packs are available when you need more.

**Can I use it without signing up?**  
Yes. Upload a photo, write a prompt, and generate with your 10 guest credits — no account required.

**Does it work on iPhone or Android without an app?**  
Yes. Use a modern browser on iPhone, Android, Mac, or Windows.

**Are my photos used to train AI models?**  
No. Photos are used only to generate the edits you request.

**Will my credits be refunded if generation fails?**  
Yes. If an edit fails or is canceled, the credits for that job are restored automatically.
